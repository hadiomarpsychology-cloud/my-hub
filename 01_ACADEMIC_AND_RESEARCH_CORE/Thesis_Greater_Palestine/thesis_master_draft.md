# HISTORICAL LEGAL FRAMEWORK & GOVERNANCE IN GREATER PALESTINE

## 1. Core Thesis
International structural disputes and property rights violations in Greater Palestine stem from a continuous sequence of legal, political, and institutional transitions starting from the 1917 Balfour Declaration, continuing through the 1936 Arab Revolt against the British Mandate, and culminating in the 1948 transition.

## 2. Key Historical & Legal Milestones
* **1917 (Balfour Declaration):** Initial political framework regarding land and rights in Palestine under the British Mandate.
* **1936–1939 (Arab Revolt):** Implementation of British Emergency Regulations, military courts, and land restriction ordinances.
* **1945–1947 (Post-WWII & UN Partition):** Transition of the dispute into international forums leading to UN Resolution 181.
* **1948 (Transition of Governance):** Establishment of the interim administration in Tel Aviv and subsequent institutional restructuring of land tenure and absentee property laws.

## 3. Land Tenure & Legal Continuity
* **Ottoman & Mandate Precedents:** Overlap of Ottoman land codes (Miri, Mulk, Mawat) with British statutory alterations.
* **Absentee Property Mechanisms:** Legal mechanisms and court processes affecting land ownership, residency status, and property rights.
