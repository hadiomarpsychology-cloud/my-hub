================================================================================
          HISTORICAL THESIS: GREATER PALESTINE LEGAL & LAND EVOLUTION
================================================================================

1. CORE HYPOTHESIS STATEMENT
   - International structural disputes and property rights violations in Greater Palestine 
     can be systematically analyzed and resolved by examining continuous legal frameworks 
     from the 1936 Revolt against the British Mandate through the 1948 transition.

2. HISTORICAL TIMELINE & INFLECTION POINTS
   - 1917: Balfour Declaration & Mandate architecture.
   - 1936-1939: The Arab Revolt in Palestine, British emergency regulations, and military courts.
   - 1945-1947: Post-WWII UN resolution framework and partition debates.
   - 1948: Transition of governance, institutional restructuring, and absentee property mechanisms.

3. LEGAL & PROPERTY MECHANISMS
   - Examination of Ottoman land tenure laws (Miri, Mulk, Mawat).
   - British Mandate statutory alterations and emergency land ordinances.
   - Subsequent custodial and absentee property legal frameworks.

4. SYNTHESIS & DOCUMENT REPOSITORY
   - Designed for clear, non-technical academic synthesis and cross-referencing 
     of historical notes, treaties, and land registry records.
