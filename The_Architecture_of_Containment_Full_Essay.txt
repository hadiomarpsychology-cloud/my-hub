THE ARCHITECTURE OF CONTAINMENT:
IDEOLOGY, LAW, ELIMINATION, AND CARTOGRAPHY IN THE GLOBAL ORDER
================================================================================

AUTHOR: Hadi Hasan Mohammed-Saleh Omar (Professorpsychologism)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This work establishes a unified theoretical framework—the Unified Theory of 
Containment—describing how imperial and state power operates not through chaotic 
or isolated acts of suppression, but through a self-referential, closed circuit. 
The system functions sequentially and simultaneously across four distinct layers: 
(1) Epistemic and Ideological Export, (2) Localized Administrative and Legal 
Translation, (3) Forensic Obfuscation and Physical Elimination, and (4) Spatial 
and Cartographic Monopolies. By analyzing Cold War intellectual pipelines, 
domestic compliance mechanisms, state-sanctioned political executions, and urban 
logistical chokepoints, this thesis demonstrates how systemic containment neutralizes 
anti-colonial resistance and preserves structural dominance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTRODUCTION: THE FOURFOLD CIRCUIT OF POWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The mechanics of global control rarely rely on blunt military force alone. When 
an imperial structure seeks to maintain dominance over an occupied or subjugated 
population, it constructs a continuous, four-tiered containment circuit:

  1. IDEOLOGY: Imperial centers engineer totalizing political philosophies, exporting 
     them to the periphery. When the colonized adapt these frameworks to demand 
     liberation, Western academic and clinical apparatuses pathologize the impulse 
     as an inherent psychological defect.
  2. LAW: While international law explicitly defends the rights of occupied 
     populations to resist colonial domination, Western domestic systems build 
     localized compliance frameworks to neutralize international mandates. 
     Simultaneously, regional client-states adopt cybercrime legislation to police 
     digital archives and suppress independent political memory.
  3. ELIMINATION: When legal translation fails to neutralize critical voices, 
     physical execution takes over. State intelligence apparatuses manipulate 
     forensic signatures—masking execution weapons under generic ballistic labels—to 
     frame assassinations as localized feuds while burying state complicity under 
     national security classifications.
  4. CARTOGRAPHY: Physical and political control requires spatial dominance. 
     Imperial power maps territory into rigid zones, controlling logistical 
     chokepoints—from maritime docks and distribution hubs to administrative 
     checkpoints—to guarantee that target populations can neither assemble nor 
     sustain independent supply networks.

This volume provides a comprehensive autopsy of this four-tiered architecture.

CHAPTER I: THE IDEOLOGICAL PIPELINE & EPISTEMIC VIOLENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 THE EXPORT OF EUROPEAN FRAMEWORKS
During the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, Western Europe served as the primary 
incubator for totalizing ideological frameworks. These included Scientific Racism, 
Social Darwinism, state socialism, fascist corporatism, and nineteenth-century 
ethno-nationalism—most notably crystallized in Theodor Herzl's 1896 text "Der Judenstaat", 
which projected European concepts of exclusive nation-state sovereignty directly 
onto the Middle East.

During the Cold War, the Eastern Bloc (primarily the USSR, East Germany, and Poland) 
institutionalized an educational pipeline designed to train Third World anti-colonial 
movements. Through institutions such as the Patrice Lumumba People's Friendship 
University in Moscow, thousands of intellectuals, cadres, and strategists from 
the Global South—including senior leaders within the Popular Front for the 
Liberation of Palestine (PFLP) and the Democratic Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine (DFLP)—were instructed in Marxist-Leninist organizational doctrine, 
guerrilla warfare, and anti-imperialist theory.

1.2 THE PATHOLOGICAL RE-FRAMING (THE AUTHORITARIAN PERSONALITY)
A fundamental structural trap was sprung when these intellectuals returned to their 
home territories to apply these European frameworks against Western colonial and 
proxy occupation. Western academic, political, and clinical institutions moved to 
pathologize the very resistance movements that European intellectual history had fueled.

Leveraging mid-century psychological metrics—specifically Theodor Adorno’s research 
on "The Authoritarian Personality" and the F-Scale—Western institutions evaluated 
anti-colonial political movements through clinical diagnostic lenses. Anti-colonial 
conviction, armed struggle, and revolutionary organization were systematically 
reframed not as rational political responses to military occupation, but as clinical 
manifestations of an "authoritarian mindset," a "totalitarian personality," or a 
"fascist impulse."

By diagnosing the victim of colonization with the psychological sickness of the 
colonizer, Western discourse executed a complete epistemic inversion: colonial 
occupation was normalized as rational, objective order, while indigenous resistance 
was classified as a collective psychological pathology.

CHAPTER II: LEGAL PLAYGROUNDS AND DOMESTIC LOOPHOLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 BYPASSING INTERNATIONAL LAW
Codified international law explicitly recognizes the legitimacy of anti-colonial 
resistance. UN General Assembly Resolution 3246 explicitly reaffirms the legitimacy 
of the struggle of peoples under colonial and alien domination and foreign 
subjugation to self-determination by all available means, including armed struggle.

To bypass this global legal consensus, Western states constructed a parallel layer 
of domestic administrative compliance codes. UK university speech policies, corporate 
ethics codes, domestic counter-terrorism strategies (such as the UK's "Prevent" 
framework), and state-level anti-boycott legislation operate below the threshold 
of international tribunals. These administrative regulations allow domestic 
institutions to penalize, censor, and prosecute anti-colonial speech and organizing 
without ever challenging international legal mandates directly.

2.2 THE TRANSLATION MANEUVER
Under this domestic paradigm, legitimate political actions are systematically 
"translated" into administrative and criminal offenses:
  - Advocacy for administrative prisoners is translated into "providing material 
    support for designated terrorist entities."
  - Economic campaigns against illegal settlement industries (the BDS movement) 
    are translated into "commercial discrimination" or "illegal trade interference."
  - Archival work, publishing historical manifestos, and distributing liberation 
    literature are translated into "disseminating extremist literature" or 
    "incitement to violence."

2.3 PALESTINE'S INTERNAL DIGITAL FRAMEWORK
Locally within Palestine, digital space underwent a parallel process of administrative 
capture. The implementation of Law by Decree No. 16 of 2017 on Cybercrime, 
subsequently revised as Law No. 10 of 2018, established a rigorous domestic security 
framework over digital communication.

By asserting broad extraterritorial jurisdiction under Article 2, this legislation 
empowered administrative bodies to monitor digital spaces, censor unaligned political 
media, protect state-sanctioned institutions (such as the official archives of 
the Palestine Research Center), and filter independent factional databases, critical 
journalism, and unaligned digital organizing.

CHAPTER III: MECHANICS OF ELIMINATION & ARCHIVAL CONTAINMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 THE BALLISTIC AND GEOPOLITICAL MASK (THE 7.62 ANOMALY)
When epistemic pathologization and administrative legal codes fail to silence 
critical political voices, state power resorts to physical elimination. The 
assassination of Palestinian political cartoonist Naji al-Ali on July 22, 1987, 
outside the London offices of "Al-Qabas", illustrates how intelligence apparatuses 
manipulate physical and forensic evidence to manage geopolitical fallout.

Naji al-Ali was shot at close range with a Soviet-engineered 7.62x25mm Tokarev 
pistol—a specific weapon system and caliber closely associated with Eastern Bloc 
security services and militant factional operations, contrasting sharply with the 
standard .38 revolvers or 9mm sidearms used by British law enforcement.

In official statements, media releases, and judicial proceedings, the British state 
apparatus routinely generalized the weapon as a "7.62 handgun." By omitting the 
precise 7.62x25mm Tokarev chambering, the state obscured the specific forensic 
signature of the execution weapon. This deliberate generalization enabled Prime 
Minister Margaret Thatcher’s government to publicly attribute the assassination 
to an "imported foreign feud" between rival Middle Eastern factions. This official 
narrative shielded the state from investigating the full scope of Mossad’s prior 
intelligence and operational presence on British soil, culminating in the quiet 
expulsion of select Israeli diplomats while official investigative files were sealed 
under national security exceptions.

3.2 THE GLOBAL PARALLEL: PATRICE LUMUMBA AND THOMAS SANKARA
This forensic and archival management technique is a standard imperial operational template:
  - PATRICE LUMUMBA (CONGO, 1961): Following his execution by Belgian and CIA-backed 
    regional proxies, Lumumba’s body was exhumed, dismembered, and dissolved in 
    concentrated sulfuric acid to eliminate all physical evidence, prevent 
    independent forensic verification, and deny his movement a physical burial shrine.
  - THOMAS SANKARA (BURKINA FASO, 1987): Following his assassination during a 
    French-backed military coup, official state death certificates attributed his 
    death to "natural causes," while military intelligence files were classified 
    under state secrecy protocols for decades.

In every case, physical elimination is immediately paired with ARCHIVAL CONTAINMENT: 
primary evidence is destroyed, physical remains are erased, and official files 
are sealed to prevent historical and legal accountability.

CHAPTER IV: URBAN CARTOGRAPHY & THE POWER MECHANICS OF CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 THE BLUEPRINT OF TERRITORY (THE FIVE FAMILIES & HELL'S KITCHEN)
The structural containment of political movements directly mirrors the physical 
partitioning of urban space. Power relies on spatial monopolies to control logistics, 
assembly, transit, and material supply.

This principle is clearly illustrated in the historic territorial organization of 
New York City’s underworld, where the five dominant organized crime families established 
rigid spatial monopolies across key logistical axes:

  [ Hell's Kitchen / West Side Docks ] <---> [ Midtown / Finance Hub ]
  - Cuneo / Westies                          - Barzini / Genovese
  - Hudson River Piers                       - Corporate Capital
  - Rail Yards & Cargo Depots                - Construction Permits

  [ Little Italy / Judicial Center ]  <---> [ Brooklyn & Queens Docks ]
  - Corleone / Genovese-Gambino              - Tattaglia / Bonanno
  - Political / Court Influence              - Vice & Cargo Imports

  - LITTLE ITALY (CORLEONE / GENOVESE-GAMBINO SPHERE): Served as the judicial 
    and political pivot point, prioritizing municipal corruption, court manipulation, 
    and institutional continuity.
  - MIDTOWN MANHATTAN (BARZINI / GENOVESE CORPORATE ERA): Controlled modern 
    white-collar extraction, commercial real estate, garment unions, and corporate finance.
  - BROOKLYN & QUEENS WATERFRONT (TATTAGLIA / BONANNO-COLOMBO SPHERES): Maintained 
    control over cargo import docks, commercial shipping line access, and illegal 
    distribution networks.
  - NEW JERSEY WATERFRONT (STRACCI / DECAVALCANTE): Controlled industrial container 
    shipping, heavy trucking corridors, and inter-state logistics terminals.
  - HELL'S KITCHEN & THE WEST SIDE DOCKS (CUNEO / THE WESTIES): Operated as the 
    essential logistical engine room of Manhattan. By maintaining absolute control 
    over the Hudson River shipping piers, rail freight yards, and central wholesale 
    food distribution hubs (including the Fulton and Hunts Point supply pipelines), 
    the West Side apparatus controlled the physical intake of vital goods required 
    for the city’s daily survival.

4.2 SPATIAL PARTITIONING AS CONTAINMENT STRATEGY
The spatial mechanics of Hell's Kitchen establish an enduring law of territorial power:
To control a population, a sovereign entity does not need to monitor every individual 
body; it only needs to secure the logistical chokepoints.

Whether applied to maritime docks in Manhattan, military checkpoints across the 
West Bank, or digital routing nodes within national telecommunication grids, spatial 
partitioning and chokepoint control remain the final prerequisites for total structural 
containment.

CONCLUSION: THE CLOSED CIRCUIT OF POWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The four phases of containment detailed in this volume do not operate as isolated 
events; they function as a synchronized, closed loop:

  1. IDEOLOGY determines which populations and movements are epistemically pathologized.
  2. DOMESTIC LAW translates their political existence into administrative criminality, 
     bypassing global legal protections.
  3. FORENSIC ELIMINATION removes uncontainable figures, while state security 
     apparatuses classify the physical evidence.
  4. CARTOGRAPHY partitions the physical landscape, choking off logistical supply 
     lines and preventing collective assembly.

To dissect modern imperial power, one must analyze all four layers simultaneously—tracing 
the continuous thread from high political philosophy down to the concrete layout of the 
city streets.
